About Me

I am a PhD researcher in statistics at the University of Melbourne. My research focuses on multiple testing procedures, particularly false discovery rate control and replicability analysis across multiple studies.

Research Interests

Multiple testing; replicability analysis; directional inference; statistical genomics.
